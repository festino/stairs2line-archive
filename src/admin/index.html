Drag to move the selected layer. The selected layer is raised above the others. With Snap 1 px enabled, dragging and arrow keys move on the alignment pixel grid; Shift uses 10 px and Alt uses 0.25 px for deliberate subpixel adjustment. Movement is independent of view zoom, and numeric X/Y offsets keep fractional values. Mouse wheel zooms around the pointer; middle-button drag or Space + drag pans. +/− zoom, 0 resets the view, and F fits all layers. Images are rasterized at their final screen size with nearest-neighbour rendering. Pixel scale uses source-image pixels: layers at 1 have equally sized source pixels; 0.5 is convenient for a 2× pixel-art upscale. View zoom/pan and opacity are preview-only; pixel scale, rotation, flip, and position affect generated anchors.
